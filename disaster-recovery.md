# Disaster Recovery

Load this file when planning business continuity, backup, failover, or recovery.

## Requirements

- Define recovery time objectives and recovery point objectives with stakeholders.
- Identify critical services, data, dependencies, owners, and acceptable degradation.
- Document assumptions about data loss, regional failure, and external providers.

## Recovery

- Automate backups where possible and protect them from accidental deletion and unauthorized access.
- Test restores and failover regularly; a backup that has not been restored is unverified.
- Document failover, failback, communications, and escalation procedures.
- Record test results and track gaps to closure.
