# Service Ownership

Load this file when creating or operating a service, platform component, or
shared dependency.

## Service Record

Document the service purpose, repository, deployment path, environments, owners,
dependencies, data classification, and lifecycle status.

## Operations

- Define service-level indicators, objectives, alerts, dashboards, and runbooks.
- Identify primary and secondary escalation contacts and on-call expectations.
- Document backup, recovery, maintenance, and deprecation procedures.
- Review ownership and operational documentation after major changes.
