# Risk and Compliance

Load this file when work involves regulated data, audit controls, material risk,
or formal compliance requirements.

## Controls

- Classify data and identify retention, deletion, residency, and access requirements.
- Maintain separation of duties for high-risk changes and privileged operations.
- Preserve auditable evidence for approvals, deployments, access, and exceptions.
- Use approved systems for secrets, records, and sensitive operational data.

## Exceptions

- Record each exception with scope, risk, compensating control, owner, approval, and expiration date.
- Do not treat compliance controls as a replacement for engineering judgment or threat modeling.
