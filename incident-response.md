# Incident Response

Load this file for production incidents, outages, security events, or major
service degradation.

## Response

- Assign an incident commander and establish a communication channel.
- Classify severity by user impact, scope, duration, and business risk.
- Stabilize first: mitigate impact, preserve evidence, and avoid speculative changes.
- Communicate status, impact, owner, next update, and current mitigation clearly.
- Escalate when scope, risk, or required authority exceeds the responder's role.

## Recovery

- Confirm recovery with user-facing and dependency-aware checks.
- Record the timeline, decisions, contributing factors, and unresolved risks.
- Run a blameless review focused on system improvements.
- Assign corrective actions with owners, priorities, and due dates.
