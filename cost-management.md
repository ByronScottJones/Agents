# Cost Management

Load this file when planning or reviewing infrastructure and operational cost.

## Ownership

- Tag resources with service, environment, owner, and cost center.
- Assign budget ownership and review cost trends with service owners.
- Alert on unexpected spend without treating alerts as a substitute for budgets.

## Optimization

- Right-size resources using measured utilization and performance requirements.
- Use autoscaling and scheduled shutdowns where workload patterns allow.
- Review storage, data transfer, logs, metrics, and backup retention costs.
- Remove idle resources and document deliberate capacity reserves.

## Trade-offs

- Evaluate cost together with reliability, security, performance, and engineering time.
- Record material cost decisions and revisit them as usage changes.
