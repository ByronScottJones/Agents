# Accessibility

Load this file when designing or modifying user-facing software.

## Interaction

- Support keyboard operation, visible focus, logical focus order, and accessible names.
- Associate form labels, instructions, errors, and validation messages with controls.
- Do not rely on color, sound, motion, or hover alone to communicate meaning.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and provide sufficient contrast.

## Content and Testing

- Use semantic HTML or equivalent platform accessibility primitives.
- Provide meaningful alternative text and captions where needed.
- Test with automated checks and manual keyboard and assistive-technology review.
- Treat accessibility defects as user-impacting defects, not cosmetic issues.
